Label-free RL for reasoning

Co-RL: Unsupervised Reasoning Emerges from Diverse Cohort in Multi-agent RL

Training solely on self-generated feedback can reinforce existing biases and lead to training collapse. Can unsupervised reasoning emerge through cooperative multi-agent training?

01 / The problem

Labels made RL for reasoning work. Labels are also its bottleneck.

Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards is the standard route to stronger reasoning, and it needs a verified answer for every training prompt. Those answers are costly to produce and grow scarce as target capabilities pass what humans can reliably evaluate.

01 · VERIFIABLE REWARDS RLVR depends on ground-truth supervision

Annotation does not scale with the reasoning we now ask models to learn.

02 · SELF-REWARDING RL Self-rewarding derives the reward from the model's own completions

TTRL uses its majority vote, Intuitor its self-certainty, RENT its predictive entropy, and Co-rewarding its agreement with a copy of itself.

03 · THE FAILURE MODE The signal never leaves the model

Self-reinforcement amplifies existing biases and can end in homogeneous outputs or training collapse.

02 / The idea

Co-RL takes the reward from a peer, never from the model itself.

Independently trained models make different errors. Each one can therefore correct mistakes the other cannot see in its own generations. Co-RL turns this into supervision, and each agent is rewarded when its answer matches a peer's majority vote.

STEP 01 Sample and vote

Each agent samples several completions for the same unlabeled prompt and reduces them to a majority vote.

STEP 02 Reward against the peer

A completion earns reward 1 when its answer matches the peer's vote. An agent never contributes to its own supervision target.

STEP 03 Update independently

GRPO updates each policy separately. The agents share no parameters and exchange no gradients.

03 / The result

Trained with zero labels, it matches training with the labels.

The hardest comparison for a label-free method is the same recipe trained on the real answers. GT-Reward holds the models, data, and budget fixed and adds the ground truth. Co-RL matches or surpasses it in 11 of 14 settings and matches or beats TTRL in 13 of 14.

04 / Why it works

What a cohort teaches depends on how different its mistakes are.

Highly similar models make correlated errors and can reinforce the same incorrect answers. The paper therefore pushes diversity through three channels, and each one is measurable.

Decoupled policy optimization

Each policy keeps its own parameters and optimizer state, and the only interaction is the exchanged vote. Panels (b) and (c) below show that this alone stabilizes training relative to self-rewarding.

Different model families and sizes

Families differ in architecture, tokenization, and pretraining data, and vision-language families also differ in their vision encoders. How much two models' errors overlap can be measured before any training, on 500 MATH problems per pair.

The finding Every different-family pair ends at or below κ 0.42 and every same-family or seed-only pair at or above κ 0.51. No pair lands between the two groups. Crossing families lowers the average overlap from 0.53 to 0.38, and the downstream gains in section 03 follow the same ordering.

Decoupled input formation

The Different family+ runs train one agent on the original MATH prompt and the other on a DeepSeek-V3 rewrite. The rewrite recasts the problem in a new scenario and never changes the answer.

07 / Run it

Every setting on this page is one launcher away.

The release is Apache-2.0 and ships the baselines next to the method. The exact paper configuration is documented in the repository.

# quickstart
git clone https://github.com/DrStranded/Co-RL && cd Co-RL/llm
pip install -r requirements.txt && python tools/verify.py

# the settings, as launchers. Swap MODEL_A / MODEL_B to move between them.
bash examples/ttrl_qwen25_1p5b.sh                                # baseline: TTRL
bash examples/same_family_qwen25_1p5b.sh                         # Co-RL (Same family)
bash examples/different_family_qwen25_1p5b_x_llama32_1b.sh       # Co-RL (Different family)
bash examples/n3_ring_qwen25_1p5b_x_llama32_1b_x_qwen3_0p6b.sh   # three-agent ring
cd ../mllm && bash examples/different_family_qwen25vl3b_x_internvl35_2b.sh  # vision-language
